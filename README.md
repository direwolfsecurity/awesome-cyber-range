# awesome-cyber-range

Hardware, software and resources for building a cyber range on a school budget. Collected over a few decades of building them.

Free and open source first. Paid items are marked **($)**.

## Hardware

- [Cases2Go](https://www.cases2go.com/) **($)**: rolling rack cases, for a range that has to move between rooms.
- [StarCase rack frames, 10-32 tapped steel](https://starcase.com/product/rack-frames-10-32-tapped-steel/) **($)**: bare rack frames for building your own cases.

## Range platform

- [Proxmox VE](https://www.proxmox.com/en/proxmox-virtual-environment/overview): the hypervisor the range is built on.

## Grey cell: the fake internet

- [step-ca](https://smallstep.com/docs/step-ca/): a private certificate authority with ACME, so the range has its own "Let's Encrypt".
- [TileServer GL](https://github.com/maptiler/tileserver-gl): serves OpenStreetMap tiles offline.
- [Kiwix](https://kiwix.org/): offline Wikipedia and documentation for an air-gapped room.

## City and business services

Open-source apps that give the city's businesses something real to run (and to attack and defend).

- [OpenEMR](https://www.open-emr.org/): electronic health records, for the hospital.
- [OrangeHRM](https://www.orangehrm.com/): human resources and employee records.
- [Sahana](https://sahanafoundation.org/): disaster and emergency management, for the city's emergency office.

## Defense and forensics

- [Kansa](https://github.com/davehull/Kansa): PowerShell incident response collection across many Windows hosts.
